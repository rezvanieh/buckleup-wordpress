# BuckleUp Driving School — SEO Blog Plan (10 new articles)

Editorial rationale for the 10 new posts in `content/blog-seo/`. These do **not** overlap the
5 existing posts (`how-to-pass-icbc-class-5-road-test-vancouver`, `mastering-parallel-parking-ultimate-guide`,
`winter-driving-bc-essential-safety-tips`, `why-port-moody-best-place-learn-to-drive`,
`ultimate-highway-merging-checklist`). Together with those, they form four internally-linked topic clusters
so search engines see BuckleUp as a topical authority on learning to drive in Metro Vancouver.

All articles are accurate for BC/ICBC as of June 2026. Where exact fees or test rules can change, the copy
stays general and points readers to **ICBC.com** — notably the **Graduated Licensing Program (GLP) changes
arriving in summer 2026** (removal of the second road test for clean-record Novice drivers), which several
articles flag without inventing a precise effective date.

## Topic clusters

- **Getting Started / Licensing** (pillar: GLP explained): articles 1, 2, 5, 10
- **Choosing a School / Commercial intent**: articles 3, 4, 8
- **Road Test Routes / Local**: articles 6, 7 (link to the existing Class 5 pass guide)
- **Differentiator / Local**: article 9 (Farsi-speaking lessons)

Every article links to a mix of `/services`, `/instructors`, `/contact`, the relevant
`/locations/{…}` page(s), and 1–3 sibling posts (new or existing). Closing CTA is always WhatsApp
(`wa.me/16044413677`) and/or `/contact` — no booking-app UI (v1 is marketing-only).

## Accuracy & sourcing notes
- Knowledge test = 50 multiple-choice questions from *Learn to Drive Smart*; exact pass mark and fees are kept
  general and deferred to ICBC.com (fees change).
- GLP: 7L (min 12 months, supervised) → 7N (Novice, restrictions) → Class 5. The article flags the **2026 GLP
  changes** (clean-record Novices skipping the second road test) and tells readers to confirm current rules on ICBC.com.
- New residents may drive on a valid out-of-province/out-of-country licence for up to **90 days**; reciprocal
  jurisdictions can exchange without testing, others test. We list examples but defer the authoritative list to ICBC.com.
- Test centres referenced: **Port Coquitlam (1930 Oxford Connector)** and **North Vancouver (1331 Marine Drive)**.
  Routes are described in general terms (we do not claim a fixed examiner route).
- No precise dollar fees are asserted as ICBC fact; market lesson prices are given as ranges and clearly framed as estimates.
